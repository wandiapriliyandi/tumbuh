# Quality Traceability and Governance

**Status:** DESIGNED — v2.0.0 / Assessment Quality
**Epistemic status:** Conceptually specified; empirically provisional.

## 1. Purpose

Quality claims harus dapat ditelusurkan sehingga pembaca yang berwenang dapat mengetahui construct, intended use, evidence, dan review yang menjadi dasar klaim tersebut.

## 2. Traceability Chain

```text
Construct ID
   ↓
Intended Inference
   ↓
Instrument / Rubric
   ↓
Evidence
   ↓
Quality Review
   ├── Rater Calibration
   ├── Bias Management
   └── Validity / Reliability
   ↓
Scoped Interpretation
   ↓
Decision
```

## 3. Minimum Quality Record

Record quality sebaiknya memuat:

- construct;
- intended inference;
- intended use;
- population/context;
- assessment method;
- evidence reviewed;
- calibration information bila relevan;
- bias/fairness review;
- validity evidence;
- reliability evidence;
- limitations;
- quality status;
- reviewer dan waktu review bila diperlukan.

## 4. Governance

Governance perlu menjaga agar:

- klaim quality tidak melampaui evidence;
- perubahan instrument atau procedure tercatat;
- version changes dapat ditelusuri;
- keputusan sensitif memiliki human oversight;
- privacy dan data minimization tetap berlaku;
- quality status dapat ditinjau kembali.

## 5. Claim Registry

Klaim seperti “validated”, “reliable”, atau “fair” harus memiliki status epistemik dan evidence yang dapat ditelusuri.

Design status tidak boleh diperlakukan sebagai empirical support.

## 6. Digital / AI Governance

Untuk sistem digital atau AI, traceability juga perlu memperhatikan:

- provenance data;
- system/model version;
- processing step;
- output yang digunakan;
- human review;
- error correction;
- privacy dan access control.

```text
AI Output
   ↓
Source Check
   ↓
Human Review
   ↓
Scoped Use
```

## 7. Reopening

Quality architecture perlu ditinjau kembali bila terdapat:

- evidence baru yang material;
- perubahan intended use;
- perubahan population/context;
- perubahan instrument atau system;
- repeated boundary failure;
- masalah fairness, privacy, safeguarding, atau traceability.

## 8. Boundaries

Traceability bukan:

- bukti bahwa klaim otomatis benar;
- sertifikat validity universal;
- pengganti empirical evaluation;
- alasan menyimpan semua data;
- automatic decision mechanism.

**Decision:** Quality governance makes assessment-quality claims auditable, scoped, and revisable without converting quality assurance into a permanent guarantee.
