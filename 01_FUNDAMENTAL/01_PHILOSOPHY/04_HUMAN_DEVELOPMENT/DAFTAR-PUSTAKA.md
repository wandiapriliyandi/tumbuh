# Daftar Pustaka — Human Development

Daftar pustaka ini memuat sumber yang digunakan sebagai dasar pembahasan dalam dokumen Human Development. Sumber dapat berasal dari literatur perkembangan manusia, psikologi perkembangan, pendekatan relasional, dan sumber lain yang relevan dengan batas pembahasan Foundation TUMBUH.

## Perkembangan manusia dan pendekatan relasional

1. Baltes, P. B. (1987). Theoretical propositions of life-span developmental psychology: On the dynamics between growth and decline. *Developmental Psychology*, 23(5), 611–626.
2. Lerner, R. M. (2004). Diversity in Individual↔Context Relations as the Basis for Positive Development Across the Life Span. *Research in Human Development*, 1(4), 327–346. https://doi.org/10.1207/s15427617rhd0104_5
3. Lerner, R. M., Brindis, C. D., Batanova, M., & Blum, R. W. (2018). Adolescent Health Development: A Relational Developmental Systems Perspective. Dalam *Handbook of Life Course Health Development*. NCBI Bookshelf.
4. Lerner, R. M., Lerner, J. V., & Benson, J. B. (2011). Positive youth development: Research and applications for promoting thriving in adolescence. Dalam *The Oxford Handbook of Positive Organizational Scholarship*.

## Plastisitas dan variasi perkembangan

5. Baker, A. E., Galván, A., & Fuligni, A. J. (2025). The connecting brain in context: How adolescent plasticity supports learning and development. *Developmental Cognitive Neuroscience*, 71, 101486. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.dcn.2024.101486
6. Sisk, C. L., & Gee, D. G. (2022). Stress and adolescence: Vulnerability and opportunity during a sensitive window of development. *Neuroscience & Biobehavioral Reviews*.
7. Hülür, G., Gerstorf, D., & Ram, N. (2015). Cognitive training and plasticity: Theoretical perspective and methodological consequences. *Frontiers in Human Neuroscience*, 9, 107. https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3607292/

## Perspektif rentang kehidupan

> **Catatan:** daftar ini berfungsi sebagai daftar pustaka induk Human Development.
