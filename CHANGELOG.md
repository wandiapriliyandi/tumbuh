## [1.0.0] - 2026-08-21 (Full Release — Model Pembinaan Pesantren Terpadu 24-Jam)

### 🌟 Ringkasan Rilis Utama
Rilis perdana komprehensif ekosistem **TUMBUH** yang merampungkan **11 Domain Arsitektur Utama**, **21 Custom Skills Dewan Keilmuan**, dan sistem pengasuhan terpadu 24-jam tanpa dikotomi madrasah-asrama.

### 🏛️ Modul & Domain yang Dirilis:
1. **01 Philosophy**: 11 Sub-Domain Falsafah Ontologi Fitrah, Epistemologi Turats, & Meta-Naratif.
2. **02 Principles**: 10 Sub-Domain Prinsip Pembinaan, *Firm & Kind*, & Zero Corporal Punishment.
3. **03 Capacity Framework**: 10 Sub-Domain Kapasitas 10 Muwashafat Karakter Santri.
4. **04 Progression Framework**: 11 Sub-Domain Trajektori Perkembangan Biososial-Spiritual & Jenjang J1–J4.
5. **05 Assessment Framework**: 11 Sub-Domain Asesmen Ipsatif, Validasi Psikometri, & Portofolio.
6. **06 Intervention Framework**: 11 Sub-Domain SW-PBIS Multi-Tier (Tier 1-3) & Disiplin Restoratif.
7. **07 Implementation Framework**: 11 Sub-Domain Struktur Terpadu 24-Jam (Kepala Pesantren $\rightarrow$ Kepala MA/MTs $\rightarrow$ 4 Wakamad $\rightarrow$ Staf Pelaksana).
8. **08 Integrated Approaches**: 7 Sub-Domain Pendekatan Terintegrasi (PBIS, SEL, Mentoring, Coaching, Positive Discipline, Restorative Practices, Future Approaches).
9. **09 Programs**: 5 Sub-Domain Program Pembinaan 24-Jam (Core, Character, Leadership Qudwah T4, Institutional, Special Tier 2/3).
10. **10 Methods**: 7 Sub-Domain Metodologi Didaktik & Habituasi Adab (Sorogan, Bandongan, Suhbah 1-on-1, GROW, Habit Loop 66-Hari, Khidmah, Mayoran).
11. **11 Tools**: 8 Sub-Domain Instrumen & Digital Tools (Rubrik 10 Muwashafat, Logbook Musyrif App, CICO Card, BIP Form, Parent Portal App, & Database Schema).
