# Panduan Gaya Sitasi Ilmiah TUMBUH (Citation Style)

Untuk memastikan akurasi akademik dan pertanggungjawaban ilmiah:

## 1. Format Sitasi Al-Qur'an & Hadits
* **Al-Qur'an**: Nama Surah [Nomor Surah]: Nomor Ayat.  
  *Contoh:* (QS. Ar-Rum [30]: 30).
* **Hadits**: Nama Kitab/Perawi, Nomor Hadits, Tingkat Keshahihan (apabila relevan).  
  *Contoh:* HR. Bukhari no. 1385 & Muslim no. 2658.

## 2. Format Sitasi Kitab Turats
* Nama Pengarang, *Judul Kitab*, Juz/Jilid, Halaman/Bab.  
  *Contoh:* Imam Al-Ghazali, *Ihya' 'Ulumiddin*, Jilid 3, Bab Bayan 'Aja'ib al-Qalb.  
  *Contoh:* Ibnul Qayyim al-Jauziyyah, *Madarijus Salikin*, Jilid 2, Manzilah at-Taubah.

## 3. Format Sitasi Literatur Sains Modern & Studi Global (APA 7th Standard)
* **Jurnal Ilmiah Peer-Reviewed**:  
  Penulis, A. A., & Penulis, B. B. (Tahun). Judul artikel. *Nama Jurnal*, Volume(Nomor), Halaman. https://doi.org/xxxx  
  *Contoh:* Durlak, J. A., Weissberg, R. P., Dymnicki, A. B., Taylor, R. D., & Schellinger, K. B. (2011). The impact of enhancing students’ social and emotional learning: A meta-analysis of school-based universal interventions. *Child Development*, 82(1), 405–432.  
  *Contoh:* Sugai, G., & Horner, R. H. (2002). The evolution of discipline practices: School-wide positive behavior supports. *Child & Family Behavior Therapy*, 24(1-2), 23–50.  
  *Contoh:* Steinberg, L. (2008). A social neuroscience perspective on adolescent risk-taking. *Developmental Review*, 28(1), 78–106.

* **Buku & Monograf Akademik**:  
  Penulis, C. C. (Tahun). *Judul buku* (Edisi). Penerbit.  
  *Contoh:* Bandura, A. (1977). *Social Learning Theory*. Prentice-Hall.  
  *Contoh:* Zehr, H. (2002). *The Little Book of Restorative Justice*. Good Books.  
  *Contoh:* Kouzes, J. M., & Posner, B. Z. (2017). *The Leadership Challenge: How to Make Extraordinary Things Happen in Organizations* (6th ed.). Wiley.  
  *Contoh:* Edmondson, A. C. (2018). *The Fearless Organization: Creating Psychological Safety in the Workplace for Learning, Innovation, and Growth*. John Wiley & Sons.  
  *Contoh:* Clear, J. (2018). *Atomic Habits: An Easy & Proven Way to Build Good Habits & Break Bad Ones*. Avery.
