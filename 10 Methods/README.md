# 10 Methods (Kerangka Kerja Metodologi Pedagogi Pesantren TUMBUH)

Domain ini memuat petunjuk praktis dan metodologi pelaksanaan (*Pedagogical & Behavioral Methods*) di ekosistem **TUMBUH** yang terbagi dalam **7 Sub-Domain Utama**: Learning Methods, Mentoring Methods, Coaching Methods, Reflection Methods, Habit Formation, Experiential Learning, dan Collaborative Learning.

---

## Arsitektur Metodologi Pembinaan & Pembiasaan Adab

```mermaid
    MethodCore["Arsitektur Metodologi Pembinaan 24-Jam TUMBUH"]

    MethodCore --> MentorMet["2. Mentoring Methods (Suhbah 1-on-1, Peer Buddy T4, & Homesickness Care)"]
    MethodCore --> CoachMet["3. Coaching Methods (GROW Model Islami, Replacement Behavior, & Self-Regulation)"]
    MethodCore --> ReflectMet["4. Reflection Methods (Jurnal Refleksi Malam, Circle of Gratitude, & Tazkiyah)"]
    MethodCore --> HabitMet["5. Habit Formation (Habit Loop Syar'i, Nudges Visual, & Otomatisasi 66-Hari)"]
    MethodCore --> ExpMet["6. Experiential Learning (Khidmah Sosial, Rihlah Tadabbur Alam, & Role-Play)"]
    MethodCore --> CollabMet["7. Collaborative Learning (Muzakarah, Mayoran Nampan, & Restorative Circles)"]
```
