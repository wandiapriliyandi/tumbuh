# P12 — Growth Mechanism → Capacity Change Stress Test

## Temuan
Model linear Experience → Engagement → Practice → Feedback/Reflection → Adaptation → Capacity Change terlalu sederhana.

Growth Mechanism perlu mempertimbangkan prior capacity state, context, opportunity, support, relationships, environment, dan constraints. Experience ≠ growth; engagement ≠ growth; practice ≠ repetition; feedback ≠ improvement; reflection bukan universal gate; adaptation bukan satu stage.

Revised conceptual model:
```text
Context / Conditions ↕
Experience ↔ Engagement
Practice ↔ Feedback
Reflection ↔ Adaptation
        ↓
Capacity Change
        ↓
New Capacity State
        ↺
```
